FAQ

Questions teams ask before the first run.

Straight answers on what an AI operator is, how fast a pilot ships, and how much control your team keeps.

What is an AI operator?

An AI operator is a purpose-built agent that runs a specific workflow end to end: reading context across your tools, taking tool-native actions, and pausing for human approval on the steps that carry risk.

Which teams does Cognitive Layer work with?

We build operators for support, revenue, finance, and back-office teams — anywhere repetitive, tool-heavy work eats hours that should go to judgement calls.

How long does it take to get an operator running?

A contained pilot ships in seven days. We map the current workflow, build a sandbox operator against live-shaped tasks, and launch with approvals before expanding from real run data.

Which tools can an operator work across?

Operators work across CRMs, inboxes, help desks, spreadsheets, billing systems, and internal apps — including the awkward tools that have no API. If your team can do it in a browser or desktop app, an operator can usually run it.

Do humans stay in control of sensitive actions?

Yes. Every operator ships with an approval policy: refunds above threshold, external sends, and exceptions route to a human. Runs are logged with screenshots so your team can inspect exactly what happened and why.

What happens after the pilot launches?

We tune the layer weekly. Run data shows where to tighten prompts, add tools, or expand the next playbook, so the operator compounds in value instead of drifting out of date.
